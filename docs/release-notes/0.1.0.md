The first release of **Truck Save Editor**, a save editor for Euro Truck Simulator 2 that's fast, safe and pleasant to use.

### Highlights

- **Finds your saves by itself**, Steam Cloud and local, and says where it looked. Saves elsewhere can be added, or dropped on the window.
- **Your career:** money, truck and coach experience as levels with the game's rank names, skills, ADR, headquarters, company name, and your day (rest, police record, the clock).
- **Your company:** every truck, coach, trailer, garage and driver. Repair, refuel, move and remove, one at a time or picked together. Swap engines and transmissions, colour plates, make another truck or trailer yours.
- **The map:** every road on the game's map drawn from its own files, with the ones you've driven highlighted, city names and your garages. **Discover every road** explores the whole map; there's also free quick travel when the save loads, and visiting every city and unlocking every dealer and agency.
- **Statistics:** your career in numbers, and every delivery in the game's log.
- **Never lose a save:** every change listed before saving, a backup before every write, restoring in one click, nothing written while the game runs, and **Save over another** for keeping an edited copy in Steam Cloud.
- In English, German, Polish and Swedish, light or dark, and usable from the keyboard (Ctrl+K for everything).

### Good to know

- This is an early version. Most features have been checked in the game; keep the backups, and try big changes on a save you made for testing.
- American Truck Simulator saves open with a warning: much of the editor uses ETS2's data so far.
- Your World of Trucks plate is put back on your truck by the game whenever a save loads, so that truck's plate can't be changed here.
- German and Polish are first drafts; corrections are welcome.

Found a problem? **Report a problem** in Settings (or on the error message) opens a GitHub issue filled in with what helps.
